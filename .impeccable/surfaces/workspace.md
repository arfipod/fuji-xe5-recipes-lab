# X-E5 workspace

## Mode

Operate.

## Job

Read camera slots, convert an external recipe into canonical values, resolve ambiguity, choose a destination, create a recovery point, inspect the diff, write explicitly after validation and approval, and verify the result.

## Proof sequence

1. Connected camera identity and firmware.
2. Current raw and decoded values for every slot.
3. Parser provenance and confidence.
4. Current / Imported / Final comparison.
5. Backup confirmation and exact change list.
6. Immediate read-back result.
7. First power-cycle persistence result.

## Chosen direction

Camera control surface: matte black frame, white work field, compact neutral controls, structural dividers, no decorative elevation, and no color beyond functional browser rendering of external imagery and the required footer emoji.
